MarpolBase as a Knowledge Base

Yasuhiro Tanizawa

Takatomo Fujisawa

with Y. Yamamoto, T. Ohta, E. Bolton

Everything in our day-one plan — delivered.

  • Published gene, expression and literature data as RDF
  • Opened a public SPARQL endpoint, registered with RDF Portal and TogoMCP
  • Built an MCP server with 14 typed tools over the endpoint
  • Reached out to reactions, pathways and compounds through TogoMCP
  • Drove analyses end to end from natural-language prompts, no hand-written SPARQL
  • Found where the join happens: KO / GO / compound IDs, not gene IDs
